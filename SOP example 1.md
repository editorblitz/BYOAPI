## Standard Operating Procedure – *NGI Weekly Gas Market Report*

### Purpose

This document is a step-by-step guide to building, proofreading and publishing NGI’s *Weekly Gas Market Report* (NGI) newsletter. 

### Context

On Fridays before 10 a.m. ET, [Morgan Evans](mailto:morgan.evans@naturalgasintel.com)has compiled a list of stories posted over the week and sends the layout to Teresa Duberry for layout, who subsequently sends back a PDF over the weekend, along with the *Weekly Gas Price Index* PDF. Both of these are proofread and scheduled to be sent in their own newsletters to subscribers at 4:55 a.m. ET the following Monday. 

### How To Compile NGI

1. Create a document listing all stories with links posted from the previous Friday until Thursday (see an example [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10D8LnDYwEwWSYqShwXC5t1y02aK_TRNNBcU2NzWN-ZI/edit?usp=drive_link)). Title the document as “ngYYYYMMDD Vol. X No. X,” where the date is set for the Monday publication date of that NGI. 

   1. For example, if one is creating NG on the morning of Friday, May 10, they would title the document “ng20240513 Vol. 43 No. 42”

   2. Stories included in the document should have been published from Friday, May 3 \- Thursday, May 9\. 

   3. Do not include the daily market stories, storage, or any crossovers (FLXover). 

   4. Be sure to include Thursday’s *Forward Look* story listed as No. 3 in the lineup. 

2. Go into each story included in the document on the website, select Edit this page from the Wagtail bird in the lower right hand corner. 

3. Under Subscriptions, type and select NGI Weekly Newsletter for each article. Select Publish once the NGI Weekly subscription has been added. 

### How To Build NGI

Once all stories have the NGI Weekly subscription added…

1. From the [Wagtail Admin](https://www.naturalgasintel.com/ngi-editor-admin/), select Newsletter, Add Newsletter and then NGI Newsletter. 

2. Title the newsletter the same as lineup document; i.e. **ngYYYYMMDD**

3. Add in the stories from the NGI lineup document in the order you would like for them to appear.

4. Select the Newsletter Date for the following Monday. 

5. For the Email subject datetime, select the following Monday @ 4:55 a.m. ET

6. In the General Layout Notes, first, include the Title (NGYYYYMMDD Vol. X No. X – **especially the volume and issue number**). **Also note any repeat images in the lineup.** 

7. Select Save Draft

8. Double check that all desired stories have been added. 

9. Select Send Layout Email.

### How to Proof NGI: 

- [ ] The date, volume and issue number are correct on page 1, at the top of every page (just date), and in the staff box on the final page are correct

- [ ] The table of contents contains the stories you sent for that week (not all will fit, there should be text that says “And More…” at the end of the TOC

- [ ] The first two stories’ page jumps are correct and lead where they should

- [ ] The correct Forward Look article is included as story \#3

- [ ] On the final page, ensure the Weekly Spot Price Market Summary table matches the [weekly prices and trade/flow dates](https://www.naturalgasintel.com/premium/weekly-cash-market-prices/) on the website (check Henry Hub, National Avg.)

**Once the PDF has been received and proofread**

10. From the [Newsletter](https://www.naturalgasintel.com/ngi-editor-admin/pages/369/) page, select the three dots on the desired newsletter, then select Edit

11. Select Choose file and upload the NGI PDF from your computer. 

12. Save Draft

13. Review the newsletter and ensure accuracy, including that the send time is for 4:55 a.m. ET the following Monday. 

14. Select Publish after clicking the up arrow from the green bar at the bottom of the page

15. Select Send to MagnetMail.
